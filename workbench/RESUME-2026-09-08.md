## IN FLIGHT: raising The Golden Shore from "PS2" to modern

He asked: *"on a des graphismes niveau PlayStation 1 / PlayStation 2, on pourrait
élever ça pour avoir des graphismes niveau PS4 voire PS5 ?"* then *"fais ça"*.

### Already done and pushed

**The resolution law is repealed.** `world.js` used `setPixelRatio(1)` with the
comment "DPR 1 by law". Measured on a walk at 1440x900: DPR 1 costs 8.3 ms a
frame, 1.25 costs 9.7, 1.5 costs 11.5, DPR 2 costs 14.6. Quadrupling the pixels
costs 76% more time, not 400%, because the world is bound by its 3.0 M triangles
and not by fill. Now honours the device ratio up to 2, `?dpr=` still overrides,
and `stepDownPixelRatio(p95)` in world.js walks a ladder 2 -> 1.5 -> 1.25 -> 1
when the rolling p95 passes 20 ms, never back up. It is called from `notePerf`
in main.js every 4 s after a 240-frame warm-up. Measured after: DPR 2, 14.4 ms
p50, 15.8 p95.

### The survey behind the plan

- 50 `MeshStandardMaterial`, 13 `MeshBasicMaterial`. Already PBR.
- ACES filmic tone mapping, PCF soft shadows at 2048, PMREM environment.
- Only 6 normalMap and 2 roughnessMap in the whole world: the sand, plaster,
  stone and tile are smooth as plastic. This is the biggest remaining gap.
- 3.0 M triangles, 117 draw calls, 95 geometries, 35 textures.
- Roughly 2 ms of frame budget spare at DPR 2 before 60 fps is at risk.

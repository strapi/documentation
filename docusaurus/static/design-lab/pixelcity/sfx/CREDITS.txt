WAVE 4 (TOWN LIFE) - SYNTHESIZED IN THE ENGINE, NO FILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Four one-shots added with the post office, the noticeboards, the delivery
rounds and photo mode are generated at runtime by the Web Audio API in
pixelcity.js (sndSynth), not loaded from any recording:

  whoosh   - the pneumatic tube at the post office counter
             (band-passed white noise, 320 Hz -> 2.4 kHz over 380 ms)
  chime    - a delivery stamped, and the plaza plaque lighting
             (two sine partials, A5 + E6, 700 ms decay)
  blip     - a parcel taken, a note pinned, a night lantern logged
             (triangle wave, 980 -> 1400 Hz, 160 ms)
  shutter  - the postcard camera in photo mode
             (square wave, 2.2 kHz, 50 ms)
  pop      - a founding-day firework
             (low-passed white noise, 160 ms)

They are original to this build, carry no third-party rights, and obey the
same master gain and mute toggle as every recorded sound above.

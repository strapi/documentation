  HOW THAT LICENSE WAS CHECKED (round 11). Round 10 could only cite the
  December 2019 archived copy of the FMA page, and the verifier rightly
  refused to take that on trust: freemusicarchive.org answers this network
  with a 404 bot wall, and archive.today rate-limits it. So the licence was
  chased to a source that does answer, and the recording was proved to be
  the one that source describes:

    - Wikimedia Commons holds File:Kai Engel - 02 - Meekness.ogg, uploaded
      2020-04-13 from that exact FMA track URL. Its wikitext carries
      {{cc-by-4.0}}; its API returns LicenseShortName "CC BY 4.0",
      UsageTerms "Creative Commons Attribution 4.0" and LicenseUrl
      https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0 , with Kai Engel as
      author and the FMA page as source. Six years on Commons, never
      nominated for deletion. It is, honestly, still in
      "Category:License review needed (audio)" - the human review is
      pending - so it is corroboration, not a court judgment.
    - THE FILE IN THIS BUILD IS THAT RECORDING. The Commons Ogg Vorbis
      (163.5165 s) and this build's Opus (163.5230 s) match at pearson
      r = 0.999800 across 817 frames of their loudness envelopes.
      Reproducible: iterlog/r11-audition/match.py.
    - Bandcamp shows "all rights reserved" on the track page. That is the
      platform default: it shows the same on "The Run", an album whose
      CC BY status archive.org attests in machine-readable metadata. The
      footer is evidence of nothing either way.
    - The artist's own site says he is stepping away from Creative Commons
      for NEW work after 1 July 2025. A grant already made is irrevocable,
      so a 2017 release is untouched - but it is why kai-engel.com no
      longer states a licence anywhere.
    - Not a blanket claim: most Kai Engel items on archive.org are BY-NC or
      BY-NC-SA. It is per release, and this is the release that is CC BY.

  The whole trail, with every command and its output, is in
  iterlog/r11-audition/LICENCE-TRAIL.txt, along with the fully auditioned

  Used only for the trail's three rarest moments, and once each: the first
  arrival at Land's End, walking every page of one community, and
  completing the whole trail. (Round 11 corrected this line: it named two,
  while the code and the key panel have always named three - audCheckComm
  fires the theme on a completed community exactly as audLEArrive and
  audCheckComplete do.) A moment that goes by behind a muted MUSIC layer is
  not spent: the trail offers the theme again the next time.

Everything else the music layer plays is generated live in WebAudio (a small
music-box / kalimba voice written for this build) - no recorded material.

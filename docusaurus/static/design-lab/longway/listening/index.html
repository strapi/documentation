THE LONG WAY THROUGH · A ROOM OFF THE TRAIL

The Listening Room

The end-score stems did not exist. The Land’s End ensemble is not a recording: it is built from oscillators every time a moment speaks, six voices deep, and it has never been written to a file. So these were rendered — the game’s own code, driven through an OfflineAudioContext instead of the speakers.

One moment of three phrases, taken at Land’s End itself, at the golden hour (0.42), with the note seed pinned to 20260906. The mix was rendered once with every voice alive, then six more times with exactly one voice left alive and the other five stubbed out — so the six solos below are that one performance taken apart, not six different performances.

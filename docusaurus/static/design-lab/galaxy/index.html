How to read this plate

Every page of the Strapi documentation is engraved here as a star. Nothing on the plate is decoration: each mark is a measurement.

  1. A dot is a page. Its size is how many other pages cite it. The largest is Breaking changes, cited 57 times.
  2. A hairline is a real link from one page to another. There are 1231 of them, read out of the documentation itself.
  3. Position means something. Pages that cite each other are pulled together, so two neighbouring dots are two related pages.
  4. A coloured wash is a group. Where two washes overlap, the inks mix.
  5. The switch at the top is the point. As filed arranges the pages the way the sidebar files them. As cited arranges them by what actually links to what. Flip between the two and watch the documentation rearrange itself: the pages that move furthest are the ones filed in the wrong place.

Open circles at the fringe are the 50 pages nothing links to.

The Coast of Lights

the Strapi documentation, drawn as 290 lighthouses on a working sea

This is the Strapi documentation, drawn as a night coast. Every tower is a page. Every light blinks its page’s own true rhythm, read from three and a half years of history — nothing here is invented.

Drag to sail along the coast · scroll to zoom · hover a light to read its characteristic and raise its mail lanes · click it to enter the lamp room and read the page. Out on the black water the night mail is already sailing: every moving light is one real citation.

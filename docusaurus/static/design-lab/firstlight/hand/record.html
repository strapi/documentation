Put your right hand up in front of the camera, about where you would hold it to steer the chart. Then press START and follow the cues. Do each gesture the way you actually do it, not the way you think it should be done: the point is to record you.

what the tracker seesnowmeaning
hand-handedness as MediaPipe labels it, after mirroring
pinch-thumb tip to index tip, over hand size. Under 0.55 arms a grab
fist curl-middle, ring and pinky to the wrist. Under 0.95 reads as a fist
index curl-index tip to the wrist. A pinch keeps it out, a fist folds it in
aperture-index tip to pinky tip, over hand size. This is what zoom reads
lateral speed-palm, in hand widths per second. A brush needs 0.7 of one inside 0.25s
state-what the recogniser thinks the hand is doing

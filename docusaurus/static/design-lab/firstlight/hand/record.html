Sit where you normally sit

Put your right hand up in front of the camera, about where you would hold it to steer the chart, and keep your other hand on the mouse. Press START, and from then on nothing happens until you ask for it: read the instruction, start the take when you are ready, and the page waits for you again afterwards. Do every gesture the way you actually do it, not the way you think it should be done. It is you that is being recorded.

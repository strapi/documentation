MISSION BRIEFING

You are the first probe in this system. Nothing is drawn until it is measured. Every mark, number and rhythm on your instruments is read from the documentation itself.

  1. Listen. One body is transmitting at launch. Its pulse trains are real citations: one train per page that cites it.
  2. Lock on. Click a body (or search, or open the catalog with Tab) to run a surface survey. The page itself is the captured telemetry. Surveying a body triangulates its whole neighborhood onto your chart.
  3. Watch the photometer. Some bodies emit nothing: no page cites them. They are found only in transit, when they dip the light of a beacon. Intercept the dotted ellipse to make first contact.
  4. Consult the almanac. The old engraved atlas predicts where every body should sit, filed by sidebar section. Measurement disagrees: the drifted positions are flagged as ephemeris errors.
  5. Dark adapt. Dim your instruments and the commits made in the dead of night bloom cold blue-green.
  6. FULL CHART reveals the complete labeled chart at any moment. No progress is lost; your personal chart keeps only what you have actually measured.
